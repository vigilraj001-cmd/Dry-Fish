About Suvai Dry Fish

Hygienic Curing, Traditional Craft

For years, Suvai Dry Fish has been supplying premium dried seafood to families across Coimbatore district. Founded by Iqbal, our shop stands out for uncompromised focus on chemical-free preparation.

Unlike mass-produced commercial dry fish treated with heavy chemical preservatives, our products are completely organic. We wash each catch in fresh clean water, slice evenly, salt with natural rock-salt, and dry on elevated, clean bamboo mesh frames under the open sun.

Sea Salt Cured Only

Zero chemicals, food dyes, or artificial powders. Clean, high-grade organic sea salt is our only preservative.

Elevated Drying Platforms

All fish dried on clean, elevated bamboo racks protected from ground dust, pests, and insects.

Quality Inspected by Iqbal

Every batch personally inspected by the owner. If it doesn't pass our standards, it doesn't leave the shop.
